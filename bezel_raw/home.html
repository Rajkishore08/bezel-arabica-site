About Us

BEZEL ARABIA COMPANY LTD. is an ISO 9001 Certified Company, having proven its existence in the Saudi market since 1992. We are a premier Contractor of SAUDI ARAMCO, SABIC, and all its Affiliate Companies, SAUDI ELECTRICITY COMPANY, CEMENT COMPANIES, and many other National and Multi-national Companies of great repute. We are providing them Engineering, Construction, Contracting, and Maintenance services both on shore  and Off-shore. We have also been undertaking Shutdown/Turnaround Jobs, and we have an excellent track record of having executed the jobs most efficiently. We have four offices strategically located with Head Office in Jubail, Branches at Jeddah, Rabigh & Yanbu.
