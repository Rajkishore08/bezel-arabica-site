Catering Management

Catering Management

With our reliable services, we have been successful in attaining huge market appreciations. We are now recognized as a prominent caterers. Moreover, we maintain cordial relations with our clients and take various initiatives to satisfy them. Some of the reasons for which we are trusted by our patrons are:

• Purity in taste and quality of the food served
• Qualified and experienced chefs & cooks
• Rich industry experience in this domain
• Client-centric approach
• Transparent dealings
• Specialization in offering Indian and Continental cuisines

Being a quality-driven organization, we strive hard to maintain the standard of our services. While rendering our Catering Services, we take care of various factors. The food items are prepared by our experienced chefs using superior quality ingredients. Our quality controllers ensure that the dishes are prepared under hygienic condition. Moreover, we ensure that the food items are not only rich in taste but also contain nutritional values. We avoid using artificial flavors in the dishes. Moreover, we also maintain quality in the services rendered to the clients. Our well mannered staff takes care of the preference and taste of the guests.

Whatever be your catering need, no matter how big your party is – we are here to help you. We also have a home delivery section to take care of your daily needs. We guarantee you to beat our competitors in taste, service and price. We can undertake orders starting from a couple to thousands. Our menu includes several items to satisfy the taste of your guests. Our goal is to work with you and take care of your catering needs that will make you proud in front of your guests.

We offer following solutions to meet food and hospitality service requirements.

On-site Meal Solutions

Our complete culinary team manages your kitchen and ensures that you are served food that is fresh, warm and hygienic. How do we do that?

We cook on-site, following approved recipes. The Management tastes all food prior to serving, so that we know it’s good to go. We maintain the principle of cooking in smaller batches thus food is always served fresh!

At Bezel Arabia, our emphasis is not only on serving good food. We also ensure that our customers keep coming back for more at a budget that is well suited to your pocket.

 

 Off-site Meal Solutions

No space to house a kitchen? Trust us to serve you wholesome ready meals right at your doorstep, within minutes of your call.

With every business comes a need to nourish the health and performance of the people who work hard to make it suceed. The best way to ensure this is to offer a wholesome and nutritious diet. Given our expertise in serrving ready meals and great smiles, we have found the courage to venture into off-site food solutions for companies with little or no space to house a kitchen.

Give in it to Bezel Arabia experience! We innovate sensibly with newer offerings in food and catering services so that modern businesses stay unperturbed about daily nutritional needs at the workplace. Our team offer delicious meals and creative menus that appeal to a number of people beginning from energetic students to busy office goers and patients recuperating from illnesses.

 

Remote site food service

Receiving quality meals at remote job sites is often a challenge. Bezel Arabia studied best practices and conducted market research to develop a better method of providing remote catering services that focused on efficiency, without sacrificing quality. Utilizing a combination of company-owned local kitchens, food storage facilities, transport vehicles and dedicated mobile kitchen trailers for individual operating sites, Bezel Arabia has the unique capability of serving hot, freshly prepared meals to crews on-location.

We offers quality food supply, storage and catering services in remote areas. We follow our clients wherever their projects are bringing them all the good food and attention their hard working personnel need.

 We carefully select our personnel, chefs, and food so to offer a great experience in every meal. We try to bring home closer to you even if home is thousands of miles away.
